# Stanford University: Undergraduate General Education Requirements (GER)

## Overview
The General Education Requirements (GER) are designed to introduce students to the intellectual life of the University, develop essential competencies, and illustrate multiple perspectives. 

In order to graduate, undergraduates must complete four core requirements:
1. Civic, Liberal, and Global Education (COLLEGE)
2. Ways of Thinking, Ways of Doing (Ways)
3. Language Requirement
4. Writing and Rhetoric Requirement

## General Policy Statement
* **Grading Basis:** Unless specified, GER courses must be taken for a letter grade and a minimum of 3 units.
* **Language Courses:** May be taken for Credit/No Credit (CR/NC).
* **Ways Courses:** Must be taken for a letter grade unless the course is *only* offered for Satisfactory/No Credit (S/NC).
* **Creative Expression (Way-CE):** Requires only 2 units (instead of 3) and may be fulfilled with two 1-unit courses in the same department (Dance, Music, or TAPS). *Note: Prior to Autumn 2022, only CE courses could be offered for S/NC.*

## 1. Civic, Liberal, and Global Education (COLLEGE) Requirement
**Applies to:** Students matriculating in academic year 2022-23 or later.
**Requirement:** Two COLLEGE courses during the first year.

**Fulfillment Options:**
* **Standard:** Choose one course in each of two quarters (Autumn, Winter, or Spring) from the COLLEGE curriculum.
* **Residential Programs:** Take a year-long program such as *Immersion in the Arts (ITALIC)* or *Structured Liberal Education (SLE)*. These also satisfy parts of the Writing/Rhetoric and Ways requirements.
* **Autumn Exception:** Enroll in *Education as Self-Fashioning (ESF)* to satisfy one quarter of COLLEGE and PWR 1 (Writing).

## 2. Ways of Thinking, Ways of Doing (Ways) Requirement
**Requirement:** 11 certified courses providing instruction in essential skills and capacities. 
**Restrictions:** Courses taken prior to matriculation, independent studies, graduate courses, and online transfer courses are **not** eligible. A course may be certified for two Ways, but a student can only count it toward one.

**Required Distribution (11 Courses Total):**
* **Way-A-II:** Aesthetic and Interpretive Inquiry (2 courses)
* **Way-SI:** Social Inquiry (2 courses)
* **Way-SMA:** Scientific Method and Analysis (2 courses)
* **Way-AQR:** Applied Quantitative Reasoning (1 course)
* **Way-EDP:** Exploring Difference and Power (1 course)
* **Way-ER:** Ethical Reasoning (1 course)
* **Way-FR:** Formal Reasoning (1 course)
* **Way-CE:** Creative Expression (2 units minimum)*

### Transfer Credit Rules for Ways
* **Current Students:** Can fulfill a maximum of 5 Ways courses from another accredited institution. 
* **Limitations:** No more than 1 course per Way category. Max 45 total transfer units overall. Online courses are ineligible. Courses must be pre-approved prior to enrollment (max 3 pre-approval requests per term).

**Ways Requirements for Incoming Transfer Students:**
*Transfer students must satisfy remaining Ways through Stanford courses only.*

| Number of Transfer Units | Ways/GER Course Requirement at Stanford |
| :--- | :--- |
| **90** | 5 courses certified in 5 different Ways |
| **75-89** | 6 courses certified in 6 different Ways |
| **60-74** | 7 courses certified in 7 different Ways |
| **45-59** | 8 courses certified in 8 different Ways |
| **≤ 44** | 10 courses certified in 8 different Ways |

*(Note: Unit counts exclude AP/external tests, independent study, online, or additional non-approved transfer courses).*

## 3. Language Requirement
**Requirement:** One year of college-level study (or the equivalent) in a foreign language.

**Fulfillment Options:**
1.  **Coursework:** Complete three quarters of a first-year language course (4-5 units) at Stanford or an equivalent transfer course.
2.  **External Exam:** Receive a qualifying score on an approved external language exam (exams in foreign literature do not qualify).
3.  **Diagnostic Test:** Take a Stanford diagnostic test to place out entirely, or determine how many remaining quarters (1, 2, or 3) are needed. 

---

## 4. Writing and Rhetoric Requirement
**Requirement:** All bachelor's degree candidates must satisfy three levels of writing instruction.

### Level 1: First-Level Writing and Rhetoric (WR 1)
Fulfilled in one of five ways:
1.  **PWR 1:** A course emphasizing writing and research-based argument.
2.  **SLE:** Writing instruction via Structured Liberal Education.
3.  **ESF:** Writing instruction via Education as Self-Fashioning.
4.  **ITALIC:** Writing instruction via the ITALIC Integrated Learning Environment.
5.  **Transfer Credit:** Approved by the Office of the University Registrar.

### Level 2: Second-Level Writing and Rhetoric (WR 2)
Fulfilled in one of four ways:
1.  **PWR 2:** Emphasizes writing, research, and oral presentation.
2.  **SLE:** Writing and oral presentation instruction via Structured Liberal Education.
3.  **WRITE 2:** A certified course offered through a specific department/program.
4.  **Transfer Credit:** Approved by the Office of the University Registrar.

### Level 3: Writing in the Major (WIM)
* Fulfilled via writing-intensive courses designated under individual department major listings.
